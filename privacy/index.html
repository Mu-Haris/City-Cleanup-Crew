Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how City Cleanup Crew (the "App"), published by Kinex Apps ("we", "us"), handles information when you play the App on iPhone or iPad. If you have questions, contact us at muhammadharisgift1@gmail.com.

Summary

  • The App does not require an account and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your device.
  • The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect device and usage information to deliver and measure ads, as described below.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details.

Information stored on your device

The App saves your game progress and settings (for example coins, unlocked vehicles and blocks, missions, upgrades, sound and music preferences, and whether you have an active Premium subscription) locally on your device. This data is not sent to us. Deleting the App removes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the App shows banner ads during gameplay, occasional full-screen ads at natural breaks, and optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game bonus. These ads are served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC.

To deliver ads, limit how often you see them, prevent fraud and measure their performance, Google may automatically collect information such as your device model and operating system, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, app version, language, and how you interact with ads. The App does not request permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so on iOS it does not access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA); ads may therefore be less personalised.

Google's use of this information is governed by Google's own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

You can reset or limit ad tracking in your device settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and Apple Advertising). An active Premium subscription removes banner and full-screen ads; optional rewarded ads remain available if you choose to watch them.

In-app purchases and subscriptions

Coin packs and the Premium subscription are purchased through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and shares only a confirmation of the purchase with the App so it can deliver your item or confirm that your subscription is active. We do not receive or store your name, Apple ID, card number or billing address. Apple's handling of your information is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Support emails

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message (the App's support button may pre-fill your app version and device model). We use this only to answer you and do not sell or share it. You can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

Children

The App is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising and payment services described above, we do not share information about you with third parties.

Security and retention

Game data stays on your device until you delete the App. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to help you. Information collected by Google or Apple is retained according to their policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information and to object to certain processing. Because we do not hold game data on our servers, most requests concern support emails; contact us and we will respond within 30 days. For ad-related data, you can also use the controls Google provides.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App changes. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version. Significant changes will be noted in the App's update notes.
